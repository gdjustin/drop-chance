# Changelog

Everything players would notice, newest first.

## v0.8.0 · 2026-10-25 · First public playtest
- Welcome! This is the first public build. Saves stay on your device; back them up from Settings.
- Expeditions replace dungeons. In the Camp, send heroes who aren't fighting on 1, 4 or 8-hour trips. They come back with rolls on that expedition's table.
- Start with the Coin Run; chart more expedition types from the Adventure banner. Stronger teams unlock higher tiers.
- Away heroes and their gear are locked until they're back. Recalling early brings them home with nothing.
- More expedition slots and bigger teams come from the QoL banner.
- New: More → About, with this changelog, credits, save backups and a bug report button.
- The game now keeps 3 automatic save backups, and shows a recovery screen if something breaks.
- Fixed: expedition timers could read an hour short. The ticket counter now shows hero and gear tickets separately. Summon tabs sit in two rows.

## v0.7.1 · pre-release · Polish pass
- One-tap Grind mode in the bag, and a button to pull gems off unequipped gear.
- Tap a hero's gear to open it; gems now show on equipped gear.
- New bag filters: in the party, equipped without a gem, unequipped with a gem.
- The world map folds into a drawer. The Meta banner is now the Prestige banner.
- More → Get stronger scans your account and lists the levers you haven't pulled.
- Fixed: stage bar overlap, toast jitter, slot polish showing the wrong percentage.

## v0.7.0 · pre-release · The world opens up
- A drawn world map with fog that lifts as you explore.
- The Wilds: four side regions opened with Region Keys, scouted with Scouts. They stay open through a Reroll.
- Odds Shards let you pin a row you want or strike one you don't on most tables.
- The Camp: six buildings, each upgrade rolling a perk.
- Forks in the road every 20 stages: treasure, elites, shrines, merchants and the rare mythic.

## v0.6.0 · pre-release · The Reroll
- Reroll the run for tokens; tokens pull the Prestige banner for perks that raise your ceilings. Momentum makes the next run start faster.
- Normal tier for conquered regions, and boss guardians rolled from a table (watch for Gilded ones).
- The Gem banner, gem polish, slot polish, ability levels and skill books.
- The Grinder replaces salvage: every item ground is a pull on the materials table.
- More → Tables: the Codex of every table in the game, with the rows you've hit.
